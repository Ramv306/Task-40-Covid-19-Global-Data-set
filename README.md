Vaccination Impact on Fatality: Countries with higher vaccination coverage (e.g., >60%) showed a steady decline in Case Fatality Rate (CFR) during subsequent waves.
Regional Spikes: Peak surge periods varied across regions; North America saw early winter spikes while South America experienced delayed peak waves.
Rolling Averages: 7-day rolling averages effectively neutralized weekend reporting lags, providing a much clearer view of actual trajectory trends.
